# TrendHub Remote Terms of Use

_Last updated: 2026-09-17_

TrendHub provides evidence-oriented access to public trend sources and deterministic trend-analysis helpers.

## Independent service

TrendHub is **not affiliated with or endorsed by** the third-party platforms and publishers it reads. Source data may be incomplete, delayed, rate-limited, unavailable, removed, or changed by a source platform at any time. TrendHub marks missing/degraded evidence rather than guaranteeing continuous availability.

## Analytical limits

Trend lifecycle, confidence, sentiment, Source Reliability, and 24h/72h benchmark outputs are analytical indicators, **not factual guarantees**, investment advice, legal advice, medical advice, or predictions of future outcomes. Users remain responsible for verifying important decisions against primary sources.

## Acceptable use

Users are responsible for complying with applicable law and the terms of the third-party services they choose to query. Do not use TrendHub to access private data, evade access controls, harass people, or perform unlawful activity.

## Hosted-service availability

The public endpoint may enforce reasonable request-size and concurrency limits and may be changed, restricted, or withdrawn to protect reliability, security, upstream source integrity, or legal compliance. No uptime guarantee is made for third-party sources.
