# TrendHub 2.0 creator workspace

Mode: Operate. Scope: `trendhub-mcp/web/index.html` and its routed product surfaces.

Audience and task: creators and brand operators move from a live trend or business subject into an evidence-backed content project, draft, publishing preparation, and real performance review. The platform must preserve the 21-tool and evidence contracts and must not claim third-party publishing or analytics it does not possess.

## Direction contract

THESIS: TrendHub 2.0 is a creator's working shelf, not a trend dashboard. It refuses both a blank AI chat and a wall of analytics cards. Every primary surface advances a content project.

OWN-WORLD: Rice-paper workspace with restrained warm neutrals, subtle fibre texture, one maple-leaf red action color, quiet grouped sidebar, ink-dark working canvas, 10px to 12px corner system, hairline separators, Song-style display typography paired with a system sans, compact controls, and data-dense rows where comparison matters. Icons and states stay functional; decorative maple-leaf emoji is not used as interface chrome.

STORY: The user sees what needs attention, starts from a subject or live inspiration, receives a verifiable brief, writes and saves the asset, schedules the next step, and later records actual results. Trend evidence remains available without dominating the workflow.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A persistent grouped sidebar separates Creation, Trends, and System. The default workspace opens directly into a real creation project: brief fields, editable artifact, evidence status, AI connection state, and the next production action are visible without scrolling. Project history and inspiration remain secondary.

FORM: Extend the established TrendHub visual world using the supplied creator-platform recording as a hierarchy reference: global shell, grouped product navigation, page-local context, primary work, then detail drawer and actions. No visual or brand copying.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
